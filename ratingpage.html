Tripadvisor Group's new performance rating scale

The Impact rating scale provides a single, integrated view of impact, bringing together both what was delivered and how it was achieved. Leaders should evaluate overall impact by considering results, the quality of execution, and how closely the behaviors reflect our values. Strong performance requires both meaningful outcomes and the right behaviors. Sustainable impact comes from delivering results in a way that reflects how we work and lead as an organization.

Understanding the new impact scale

The Impact framework represents a shift away from the traditional 9 box, moving toward a holistic assessment of an employee's contributions. Rather than checking boxes on two separate lists, this model looks at the totality of an individual's footprint within the organization. At its core, "Impact" is the fusion of Results and Behaviors. We view these as inseparable components of professional success, not as independent variables.

The framework allows us to

Evaluate employees based on their current impact, ensuring we invest in the right talent for future growth. Provide a structured approach to assessments, promoting fairness and consistency across all teams and functions. Help leadership teams make strategic decisions about talent, succession planning, and team dynamics.
